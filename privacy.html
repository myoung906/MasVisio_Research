Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. General Provisions

Optinex Research ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information and privacy of our website visitors in compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws.

2. Information We Collect and Collection Methods

Our website is freely accessible without registration. However, if you voluntarily suggest a partnership or research collaboration via our email link, we may collect the following personal information to facilitate responding to your inquiries:

  • Required Items: Sender's email address, email content
  • Optional Items: Name, organization/company name, job title, contact details, and any other information you choose to include in your email.

3. Website Analytics and Cookie Policy

This website does not currently load a third-party analytics script and does not set cookies for visitor tracking. Standard server or hosting logs may be generated by the hosting provider for security, availability, and abuse-prevention purposes.

4. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

The collected email inquiry information is retained only while we respond to your inquiry and review a potential research or business collaboration. We review retained inquiry emails at least once every 12 months and delete information that is no longer needed, unless retention is required by applicable law.

5. Destruction Procedures and Methods

Personal information that has fulfilled its retention purpose is deleted from the relevant email or document system. If information is printed, the paper record is shredded or otherwise destroyed.

6. Rights of Users and How to Exercise Them

Users may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of their personal information at any time. You can exercise these rights by contacting us by email, and we will respond within a reasonable period after verifying the request.

7. Measures to Protect Personal Information

We limit access to inquiry emails and related personal information to people who need the information to respond to the inquiry. We do not claim operation of a dedicated intrusion-prevention or backup system unless such controls are separately confirmed and documented.

8. Privacy Officer and Contact Information

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy compliance activities, please contact us at:

- Email: info@optinex.org
