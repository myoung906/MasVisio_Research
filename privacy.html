Privacy Policy

This policy describes the limited information flow currently used by this static research website.

1. Website use

The public pages do not require registration. The website does not currently load a third-party analytics script or set cookies for visitor tracking. Standard server or hosting logs may be generated by the hosting provider.

2. Email inquiries

If you email us, we may receive your email address, message, name, affiliation, or other details you choose to include. We use them to respond to the inquiry and assess the requested research discussion.

3. Retention and deletion

Inquiry emails are reviewed at least annually. Information no longer needed for the inquiry or collaboration review is deleted from the relevant email or document system, unless a longer period is required by law.

4. Sharing and protection

Inquiry information is limited to people who need it to respond. We do not claim dedicated intrusion-prevention or backup controls that have not been separately confirmed and documented.

5. Your requests

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information by emailing info@optinex.org.

6. Important warning

Do not submit patient or research-participant personal or medical information through this website or email.

Research-stage platform. No commercial medical product is currently available.
