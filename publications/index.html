A Study on Pathogenesis Affecting Quality of Life and Daily Life of Low Vision Patients

서재명

Compared quality of life between low vision patients and normal subjects and analyzed influencing factors. Subjective life satisfaction of low vision patients was lower in all items compared to the normal control group (mean difference 36.07±14.82), and age showed a high correlation with near activity (r=-0.584) and distance activity (r=-0.678).
