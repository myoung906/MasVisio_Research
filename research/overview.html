Program 01 Readiness: Exploration

Visual Neuroscience & Low Vision Research

Representative Question

How can we isolate optical refraction deficits from cerebral visual cortex processing limitations?

Core Hypothesis

Spatio-temporal modulation of Gabor patch stimuli maps neural receptive field summation limits, reflecting neural pathway integrity and residual contrast sensitivity in low-vision profiles.

Conducted Research

Gabor patch-based spatial summation model validation, contrast sensitivity variance studies on ~50 subjects.

Current Devices & Data

Gabor stimulation control suite, dominant/non-dominant eye sensitivity dataset.

Open Challenges

Controlling longitudinal subject-to-subject baseline variability and stimulus adaptation effects.

12-24 Month Roadmap

2026.Q3-Q4: Normative cohort dataset collection. 2027.Q1-Q2: Behavior analysis library and low-vision rehabilitation module upgrades.

Request for Collaboration

Partnerships with clinical neurology departments and low-vision rehabilitation clinics.

Program 02 Readiness: Exploration

Peripheral Visual Field Metrology

Representative Question

How can we measure peripheral retinal defocus without complex clinical wavefront autorefractors?

Core Hypothesis

Inducing longitudinal chromatic aberration (LCA) using multi-point red-green flicker stimuli allows mathematical reconstruction of retinal curvature eccentricity.

Conducted Research

6-surface eye geometry ray-tracing simulations, LCA-based peripheral refraction clinical trials.

Current Devices & Data

Multi-point chromatic aberration refraction prototype, ray-tracing modeling source code.

Open Challenges

Compensating for high-order aberrations at high eccentricity (>32 degrees).

12-24 Month Roadmap

2026.Q4~: Integration with dynamic variable lens accommodating model. 2027.Q3: Release of B2B wavefront calibration toolkit.

Request for Collaboration

Joint feasibility studies with wide-field biometric hardware manufacturers and varifocal lens suppliers.

Program 03 Readiness: Prototyping

Wide-Field Corneo-Scleral 3D Aberration Prototypes

Representative Question

How do we reconstruct the combined corneal and scleral surface topology simultaneously at sub-micron precision?

Core Hypothesis

Analyzing geometric deformations in projected concentric infrared structured light patterns using differential geometry yielding sub-micron continuous mesh topography.

Conducted Research

Concentric ring pattern deformation equations, 3D anterior surface mesh reconstruction validation.

Current Devices & Data

Infrared Structured Light projector prototypes, real-time topography analysis UI.

Open Challenges

Filtering out noise caused by scleral vascular patterns and tear-film reflection variations.

12-24 Month Roadmap

2026.Q4: Deep learning vascular noise masking. 2027.Q2: Target clinical fitting error margin below 0.03mm.

Request for Collaboration

Integration partnerships with specialty scleral lens manufacturers and refractive surgery suites.

Program 04 Readiness: Clinical Validation

AI-Driven Ocular Biomarker Software

Representative Question

Can non-linear complexity reduction in Pupil Light Reflex (PLR) waveforms serve as a digital biomarker for early autonomic neurodegeneration?

Core Hypothesis

Sample entropy metrics extracted from 120fps infrared pupillometry streams across 47 dimensions correlate strongly with central nervous system fatigue and autonomic decay.

Conducted Research

47-biomarker feature extraction pipeline, anomaly detection classifier validation.

Current Devices & Data

120fps pupillometry tracking python engine, autonomic light response raw dataset.

Open Challenges

Normalizing baseline biological variance caused by subject fatigue, circadian rhythm, and medication.

12-24 Month Roadmap

2026.Q3-2027.Q1: Multi-site clinical dataset collection. 2027.Q4: Pre-regulatory filing validation for AI classifier.

Request for Collaboration

Clinical trials with neurology research hospitals and sleep disorder clinics.
