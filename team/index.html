Company / Research stage

A small research platform for whole-field vision.

Optinex Research connects ocular measurement, visual-function research, optical modeling, and prototype validation. The company is organized around questions that can be documented, reproduced, and challenged.

What we work on

Measure

Ocular and visual-function measurements under defined conditions.

Model

Field-dependent optical states and their assumptions.

Test

Prototype and validation studies with explicit limitations.
