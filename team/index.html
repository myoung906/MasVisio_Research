Company

Optinex Research measures individual ocular and visual characteristics and studies optical models and adaptive optics that extend to the periphery. Our goal is to expand human visual capabilities, building on the restoration of vision.

Our long-term research goal is full correction of peripheral retinal optics, and we explore how this could lead to vision augmentation.

Currently in research and development.

Myopia research informs peripheral optical modeling; rehabilitation research informs visual-function evaluation; and pupil research helps us understand individual responses and measurement conditions. We connect these foundations to our current direction while preserving each study’s original purpose and findings.
