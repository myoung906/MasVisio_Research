Paradigm Shift

Glasses are a single prescription. Vision is a whole field.

❌ Conventional Prescription Paradigm

  • Static prescription focusing on SPH / CYL / AXIS
  • Neglected optical defocus and peripheral field aberrations
  • Fixed lens state regardless of gaze distance or light changes
  • Lack of complex 3D retinal topography models

✅ Optinex Adaptive Vision Platform

  • Whole visual field optical profile covering center and periphery
  • Full correction of peripheral meridional curvature deviations
  • Real-time tunable optics adapting to gaze distance and PLR dynamics
  • Active feedback fitting loop based on individualized wavefront analysis

Evidence Spotlight

Peer-reviewed Study #11

Retinal Curvature & Peripheral Refraction Profile

How does peripheral refraction correlate with corneal topography and axial length?

Established peripheral curvature radius estimation models based on wide-field biometry database, analyzing spatial eccentricity deviations.

Peer-reviewed Study #14

Motion Contrast Sensitivity in Visual Pathways

How do spat-temporal frequency changes affect left/right pathways in peripheral vision?

Mapped peripheral spatial summation limits and contrast threshold using modulated Gabor patterns.

Investigator study Study #30

Shutter Glasses System for Hemianopia Rehabilitation

Does temporal time-division shutter occlusion improve mobility performance in field defect patients?

Validated rehabilitation efficacy using a custom Shutter Glasses prototype integrated with eye-tracking.

Investigator study Study #41

PLR Dynamics & Ocular Biomarkers

Can early autonomic nervous signals be isolated from pupillary response data?

Developed a 47-feature extraction pipeline and multi-wavelength LED stimulator for longitudinal analysis.
