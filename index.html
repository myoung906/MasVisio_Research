Vision Augmentation Research

Across the visual field,
expanding the possibilities of vision.

Optinex Research measures individual ocular and visual characteristics and studies optical models and adaptive optics that extend to the periphery. Our goal is to expand human visual capabilities, building on the restoration of vision.

Our Approach

Research from measurement to learning

01

Measure

Measure ocular shape, refraction, pupil responses, and visual function to understand individual differences.

02

Model

Build individual optical eye models from measurements and calculate optical conditions across the visual field, including the periphery.

03

Augment

Design ways to adjust optical conditions using the models, and investigate their potential through adaptive optics and prototypes.

04

Learn

Evaluate how optical changes affect contrast sensitivity and visual performance, then feed the findings into the next measurements and designs.
